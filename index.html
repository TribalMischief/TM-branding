Color Usage Rules
What goes where — and what never does.

On Dark Backgrounds

  • All body text → White #FFFFFF
  • Labels & eyebrows → Gold #C8A96E
  • Key values & amounts → Gold
  • Tertiary / muted text → Mid Grey

On Light Backgrounds

  • All body text → Text #1F1F1F
  • Section eyebrows → Gold #C8A96E
  • Headings → Black #111111
  • Supporting text → Mid Grey #6A6A64

Gold — Use For

  • Section labels & eyebrows
  • Key values, prices, figures
  • Divider rules, border accents
  • Closing / payoff lines

Coastal Teal — Use For

  • Links & interactive elements
  • Secondary CTAs & buttons
  • Callout panel backgrounds
  • Accent borders on teal blocks

Page Margin
Layout Principles
Six rules that keep us consistent.
Rule 01
Page Margin
72px left/right on all full-width sections. Never go below 20px. This generous margin creates our sense of editorial restraint.
Rule 02
Section Padding
56px top/bottom for standard sections. Cover and hero sections get 64–72px. Dense tables may use 32px with care.
Rule 03
Grid
12-column base. Common: 2-col (50/50), 3-col (cards), 4-col (data). Gap: 24px minimum, 48px preferred.
Rule 04
Dividers
1px solid #E8E4DE on light. 1px solid #2A2A2A on dark. Gold 3px top-border for callout blocks only. No drop shadows.
Rule 05
Text Width
Body text columns: max-width 680px for ~75 characters per line. Data tables: full width. No artificial constraints on headings.
Rule 06
Negative Space
When in doubt, add more. We communicate authority through what we leave off the page, not what we put on it.

Voice Rules
How we write — and how we don't.
We Do
  • Lead with the client's story, not our credentials
  • Use short, declarative sentences for impact
  • Name specific details — "30-year track record", "14-firm benchmark"
  • End sections with a single resonant line
  • Say the quiet part out loud — explain our thinking
  • Earn the right to be poetic with precision first
We Don't
  • "Synergistic solutions" or any agency jargon
  • Oversell with superlatives ("world-class", "best-in-class")
  • Write paragraphs longer than 5 lines
  • Use passive voice when active is available
  • Apologise in copy — we are confident, not defensive
  • Use exclamation marks in professional documents
In Practice
Same idea.
Two voices.
The difference between our voice and generic agency writing is always specificity, confidence, and restraint.
✕ Not This

"We leverage our extensive strategic expertise and best-in-class design capabilities to deliver synergistic digital solutions that drive measurable outcomes for our valued clients."

✦ This

"We help remarkable organizations look as good as they are. That starts with understanding their story — and ends with a site that actually generates leads."

Logo & Marks · 06
Two marks. Four versions.
One system.
The Tribal Mischief identity uses a geometric circle mark and a full mandala logomark — each available in white (for dark backgrounds) and dark (for light backgrounds). Never use white on light or dark on dark.

Mandala Logomark — Full Identity
Brand collateral, proposals, print, and hero use.

Dark Version
Use on: Cream #F8F6F2 · White #FFFFFF
Logo Usage Rules
Every mark. Every context. No improvising.
✦ Correct
Do This
White mark on Black (#111) or Text (#1F1F1F)
Dark mark on Cream (#F8F6F2) or White (#FFF)
Clear space = cap-height on all four sides
Scale proportionally — lock aspect ratio always
Circle mark at small sizes (16px+), mandala at 48px+
✕ Never
Not This
White mark on Cream, Gold Light, or any pale bg
Dark mark on Black, Text, or any dark surface
Recoloured or gold-tinted versions of either mark
Placed over busy photography without a solid backing
Stretched, skewed, or rotated out of upright position
